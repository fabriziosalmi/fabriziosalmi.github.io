Privacy & Legal Notice

Last updated: 21 July 2026

What this covers. This single notice applies to all the open-source project sites published under fabriziosalmi.github.io. They are static documentation sites served from one host, with the same publisher and the same technical setup, so one notice describes them all rather than dozens of copies drifting apart.

English

Who publishes these sites (Data Controller)

Fabrizio Salmi — Genoa, Italy. These are personal, non-commercial open-source project sites.
Contact for any privacy request: fabrizio.salmi@gmail.com.

What data is processed, and why

  • Your IP address and basic request data (user-agent, page requested) are processed transiently by the hosting provider in order to deliver the page and to protect it against abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not store this data ourselves and we never receive it in identifiable form.
  • No analytics. These sites run no analytics, no tracking pixels and no advertising scripts — neither first-party nor third-party.
  • No accounts, no forms, no newsletter. No name, email or other identifying information is collected from visitors.

Cookies & device storage

These sites set no cookies, so no cookie-consent banner is required.

Some of the documentation sites remember your light/dark appearance preference in localStorage (for example under the key vitepress-theme-appearance). That value is functional: it exists only because you chose a theme, it stays on your device, and it is never transmitted to us or used to recognise you.

Processor & international transfer

GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts these pages through GitHub Pages and is the only processor involved in serving them. Delivering a page therefore transfers technical data such as your IP address to the USA; that transfer is covered by GitHub's Data Processing Agreement and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework / Standard Contractual Clauses. There are no other transfers.

Third-party resources

These sites are built to be self-contained: fonts, styles and scripts are served from the same host. A small number of project pages still embed a resource published by a third party — for example a build-status badge or a library from a public CDN. Where that happens, the request travels directly from your browser to that provider, which will see your IP address. Such embeds are being progressively removed.

Retention

We keep no personal data. Any technical logs held by the hosting provider are transient and retained only as long as needed for delivery and security.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (Arts. 15–22 GDPR). Since we hold no personal data about you there is normally nothing to act on, but you may write to fabrizio.salmi@gmail.com at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

No profiling

No profiling and no automated decision-making concerning visitors is carried out.
